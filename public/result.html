Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add my new email signature to Outlook (Windows & Mac)?

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. Open Outlook and start a New Email.
  3. On the Message tab, choose Signature > Signatures… (on Mac: Outlook > Settings > Signatures).
  4. Click New (or +), give the signature a name, and paste into the editing area.
  5. Under Choose default signature, pick this signature for New messages and, if you like, Replies/forwards.
  6. Click OK / Save. Your signature is now inserted automatically.

How do I create a signature for Outlook on the web and Outlook.com?

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. Sign in to Outlook on the web and click the Settings cog (top-right).
  3. Go to Mail > Compose and reply.
  4. Click New signature, give it a name, and paste into the editor.
  5. Under Select default signatures, choose it for new messages and replies/forwards.
  6. Click Save.

Tip: if images look missing in the editor, send yourself a test email. Outlook loads the icons and logo from the web when the message is viewed.

How do I add my new email signature to Apple Mail (macOS)?

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. Open Mail and choose Mail > Settings… (or Preferences…), then the Signatures tab.
  3. Select your email account in the left column and click + to create a new signature. Give it a name.
  4. Click into the signature box on the right, delete the default text, and paste (⌘V).
  5. Untick Always match my default message font so the signature keeps its own styling.
  6. Use the Choose Signature menu at the bottom to make it the default, then close the window.

iPhone and iPad

The Mail app on iOS keeps signatures as plain text, so logos and icons cannot be added there. Send yourself an email from your Mac with the new signature, open it on the phone, select and copy the signature, then paste it in Settings > Mail > Signature. Formatting will be simplified.
